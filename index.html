Island Panic: Building Survival - Privacy Policy

Publisher: NEXORALABS
Last updated: 6 November 2026

Island Panic: Building Survival ("the game") is an offline single-player game.

Data we collect

None. The game does not collect, transmit or share any personal information. It does not use the internet, accounts, advertising, analytics or tracking.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your settings (language, interface size, fullscreen), progress and achievements in a local file on your device. This file never leaves your device and is removed when you uninstall the game.

Children

The game does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Third parties

The game contains no third-party advertising or analytics services. It is built with the Godot Engine (MIT license).

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address.

Contact

Contact NEXORALABS through the game's Microsoft Store listing.
